## Problem

Most small businesses reply to a new lead hours later, or not at all. By the
time someone gets back to them, the lead has usually spoken to a competitor —
and the owner's time goes to triaging an inbox instead of to the leads that
were ever going to buy.

## Solution

One workflow handles the whole first touch. Every lead gets a personal reply
that mentions what they actually asked about, every lead is logged with its
score and the reason for it, and the owner hears about a lead only when it
scores high enough to be worth a conversation.

## How it works

<Architecture />

The lead arrives from the techenzo.com contact form, sent server to server so
the webhook URL never reaches the browser. The shared secret travels in a
header that the Webhook node checks, because anyone who found an open URL
could otherwise spend AI credits and send real email.

One model call does both jobs — score and reply — and the structured output
parser is what makes that safe: the downstream nodes read named fields, not
whatever the model felt like writing.

## Status

The workflow runs end to end against test leads. What is still to come: real
traffic through the site's contact form, and a Lab write-up with the measured
response time, cost per lead, and where the model's scores disagreed with a
human's.
